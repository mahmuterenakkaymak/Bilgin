# Visual review — M21 (ask/answer surface, clarification, exports, saved + history, rules, users, map, dark mode)

## Real problems
1. **Masthead nav · 390** — the phone nav is a deliberately scrolled row (`overflow-x: auto; scrollbar-width: none`, web/src/styles.css:962) but nothing says so: "Veritabanı haritası" prints as "Veritabanı harita", and because the row auto-scrolls to the current page the Kullanıcılar frame opens with the row starting at "Veritabanı haritası" — "Sor" and "Kurallar" are simply gone (users-screen-users-table-390.png, rules-screen-validation-390.png). A phone user has no way to know there are four destinations, or that the primary one is one flick away.
2. **Answer card · evidence · both widths** — Evidence.jsx:135 marks every `ok:false` step with a red ✗ plus the tool name and the raw summary, so the panel that promises "how you got this number" reads "✗ get_schema — Bilinmeyen araç get_schema. Kullanıcıların: search_schema, get_table_details, find_join_path, …" (ask-golden-answer-chart-evidence-open-390.png). The answer succeeded; the panel says otherwise, in the model's own tool vocabulary, and `run_sql` and `submit_sql` then print the same pipe-delimited row dump the table above already shows.
3. **Map report · both widths** — "Kişisel veri 1" is the one number that should stop an ERP admin before a workbook becomes a queryable dataset, and it is the sixth identical neutral tile next to "Referans 0" (admin-map-onboard-map-report-390.png, -1366.png); "Kapalı" and "Referans" are bare single words with no sentence anywhere saying what they count.
4. **Users · 1366** — the signed-in admin's own row carries "Pasifleştir" (web/src/components/UsersScreen.jsx:286) and one click closes their session, and the Rol select commits on `onChange` (UsersScreen.jsx:263) with no confirm and no undo. A stray click locks the only admin out of the app.
5. **Masthead dataset chip · 390** — `#model-chip` is capped at `max-width: 7rem` (web/src/styles.css:861), so the header states the active dataset as "Sentetik ERP (…" (landing-empty-state-390.png) and "ornek_fatura ·…" after a switch (admin-map-onboard-map-name-taken-390.png). Which dataset a number came out of is the one thing this header exists to say, and at phone width it says none of it.

## Craft ideas (cheap, clearly better)
6. Toplam / En yüksek / Ortalama / En yüksek pay are computed deterministically and shown only inside the collapsed panel (Evidence.jsx:122) — two or three chips under the answer sentence would make the card readable at a glance instead of one row count and a table to add up by eye.
7. The map wizard's three numbered cards are all open at once and never change state: after "Oluştur" succeeds, "1 · Şablonu alın" looks exactly as untouched as it did at the start (admin-map-onboard-map-done-390.png). A done/current mark on the three numbers would make a three-step job feel like it moved.

## Checked, not problems
The 401 lines are the pre-login `/api/auth/me` probe; the mid-page header and the black band are sticky-header stitching. "Satır sayısı 5." is the fake narrator speaking the first fact's own display (nl2sql/agent/fake.py:231), not the real narration or its fallback. The dark-mode "Sor" button is `--accent: #6fa8e8` on `--on-accent: #10233b` by design (styles.css:34). The CSV/Excel mismatch in export-buttons-after-export is the 2 s "İndirildi" reverting between the two scripted clicks. The 1366 timeouts are the intentionally hidden `.sidebar-toggle` (styles.css:1297) and `ornek_fatura` already existing from the 390 pass on the shared server; every other TimeoutError is a page whose `from` gate (M23/M24/M25) is not ticked yet, so its selectors are skipped. What is genuinely good: the clarification card at both widths; the rules form's per-field errors with the "3 alan hatalı" tally; the read-only banner with the buttons actually gone; the map's Sayfa/Satır/Kolon/Sorun table at 1366 and its stacked equivalent at 390; "İndirildi" and "Kaydedildi" as button states; the ✓ Yazılıyor / İndeks kuruluyor / Hazır track.
